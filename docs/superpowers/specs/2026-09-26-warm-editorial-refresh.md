# Estudios Estrada Warm Editorial Refresh

**Date:** 2026-09-26  
**Status:** Approved for implementation

## Goal

Refine the existing modular website so it feels like a premium photography and audiovisual company: editorial, warm, visual, and organized without depending on an all-black presentation or pretending generated imagery is company work.

## Approved direction

- Use warm ivory, cream, sepia, and restrained metallic gold as the dominant visual language.
- Keep black and charcoal as contrast accents, primarily in the final call to action.
- Use a light header and footer with the supplied dark Estudios Estrada logo.
- Keep the existing separate routes and service-led navigation.
- Preserve the text-only animated film rail, with no photographs inside its frames.
- Make the home hero editorial and image-conscious using the real brand mark, framing, grain, and light effects until approved company photographs are supplied.
- Present service cards as refined editorial film frames rather than dark placeholders.
- Present the work process on a light surface with fine separators and gold numbering.
- Make the company preview feel like an editorial spread using the real logo and abstract film details.
- Keep the last home call to action dark so the contrast is intentional and limited.

## Content and integrity constraints

- Do not embed the supplied composite concept render into the production site.
- Do not present AI-generated people or scenes as Estudios Estrada portfolio work.
- Do not invent years of experience, client counts, awards, locations, delivery promises, prices, or other claims.
- Keep “Consultar disponibilidad” as the primary action; do not add a quotation calculator.
- Preserve keyboard support, responsive behavior, reduced-motion behavior, and readable contrast.

## Acceptance

- The home page is predominantly light and warm from the header through the company section.
- Black no longer dominates the header, hero, service cards, process, or footer.
- The film-strip identity is visible in the hero, service cards, and animated rail without making every section visually identical.
- Mobile and desktop layouts have no horizontal overflow and all CTAs remain reachable.
- Existing automated checks and the production build pass.
